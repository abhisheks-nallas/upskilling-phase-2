# Benefits and Payroll

## Salary dates
Salary is paid on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available in the HR portal on the same day. If your joining date is after the 15th of a month, your first salary is paid together with the next month's salary.

## Health insurance
Every employee, their spouse and up to two children are covered by group health insurance of INR 5,00,000 from the first day of employment. Parents can be added at a small extra premium during the annual enrolment window in February. The insurance card is emailed within 10 days of joining.

## Provident fund
Both you and the company contribute 12 percent of basic salary to the provident fund. Your UAN number from any earlier job must be given to HR in your first week so that the account can be linked.

## Referral bonus
If someone you refer is hired and completes 6 months, you receive a referral bonus of INR 25,000. Referrals are submitted in the HR portal before the candidate interviews, and a referral cannot be added after the fact.

## Payroll questions
For payslip or tax questions, email the payroll team or raise a ticket in the HR portal. Declare your tax-saving investments by 31 January each year, because payroll cannot change deductions after that.
