# Client Projects and Communication

## Working with client data
Customer data stays inside the client's environment unless the contract says otherwise. Do not copy production data to your laptop, and use masked or synthetic data for development and testing. If a debugging task seems to need real data, ask the delivery lead, who will get written approval from the client. Screenshots that show customer data must not be posted in internal chat channels.

## Daily and weekly rhythm
Most project teams hold a 15-minute stand-up at 10:30 IST every day. The weekly status report goes to the client every Thursday by 17:00 IST, written by the delivery lead from the notes each team member adds to the project tracker by Wednesday evening. Keep your notes short: what was done, what is planned and what is blocked.

## Client calls and email
Reply to client emails within one working day, even if the reply only says when you will have an answer. Keep calls on time, join two minutes early and keep your camera on. Do not agree to a change of scope, a new deadline or a price in a call. Say you will check with the delivery lead and follow up in writing. Copy the delivery lead on any email that contains a commitment.

## Escalating a problem with a client
If a client is unhappy or a deadline is at risk, tell your manager and the delivery lead the same day. Do not wait for the weekly report. They decide whether to bring in the account manager. Late bad news is the problem that is hardest to fix, and raising a risk early is never held against you.

## Using AI tools on client work
Do not paste client code, documents or customer data into public AI chat tools. Use only the approved assistants listed on the security page, and check that the client contract allows AI assistance on their code. AI-generated code is reviewed like any other code, and you remain responsible for it.

## Time tracking for billable work
Billable hours go in the timesheet against the client project code, and non-billable work such as training or internal talks goes against the internal code. Wrong codes affect client invoices, so check your entries before you submit on Friday.
