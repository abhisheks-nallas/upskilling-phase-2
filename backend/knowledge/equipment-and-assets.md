# Equipment and Asset Policy

## Laptop refresh and upgrades
Laptops are refreshed every 4 years. A developer who needs more memory can ask for an upgrade to 32 GB of RAM before the refresh, with a short justification approved by the tech lead and then by IT. Upgrades are done by the IT desk on the ground floor and take about two working days. Your old data is moved for you, but keep important files in the shared drive anyway.

## Monitors and accessories
Everyone gets one monitor, a keyboard and a mouse for the desk at the office. A second monitor can be requested for work that needs it, up to INR 12,000, and the purchase is made by IT, not by you. Headsets are provided on request. Chairs and desks at the office are managed by facilities, and ergonomic problems should be reported to them.

## Lost or stolen equipment
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT hotline and to the security team within 2 hours of noticing. IT then locks and wipes the device remotely. You must also file a police complaint within 24 hours and upload a copy in the helpdesk ticket. If the loss is found to be due to negligence, such as leaving a laptop in a car, you may be asked to pay up to 25 percent of the depreciated value of the device.

## Accidental damage
Accidental damage is covered by company insurance once every 2 years for each device. A second incident within 2 years means you pay 50 percent of the repair cost. Damage from misuse is never covered. Take the device to the IT desk and do not try to repair it yourself or through a shop, as that can void the insurance.

## Personal devices
Personal laptops must never be used to access production systems or customer data. Personal phones can be used for company email and chat only after the device management profile is installed, and IT can remove the company data from the phone if you leave or lose it. Storing customer data on a personal phone is not allowed.

## Returning equipment when you leave
Return the laptop, accessories and access cards to IT on your last working day, and get a signed return receipt. If anything is not returned, its depreciated value is deducted from your final settlement.
