# Expenses and Travel

## Claiming expenses
Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense, with the original receipt attached. Claims older than 30 days are rejected unless your manager writes an exception. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Limits
Claims up to INR 5,000 are approved by your manager. Anything above INR 5,000 also needs finance approval, and claims above INR 25,000 need prior written approval before you spend the money. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Business travel
Book travel through the company travel desk, not on your own. Domestic flights are economy class, and hotels are capped at INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 elsewhere. Travel needs manager approval at least 7 days before the trip, except for urgent client visits.

## Daily allowance
When you travel for work you get a daily allowance of INR 1,200 for meals and local transport, paid without receipts. Meals that are already included in a client event are deducted from the allowance.

## Phone and internet
Employees on roles with regular client calls get a monthly reimbursement of INR 1,000 towards mobile and home internet. Claim it once a month with a bill.
