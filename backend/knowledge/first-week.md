# Your First Week

## Day 1
Arrive at 10:00 at reception and ask for HR. You get your laptop and access card, and complete the HR paperwork before lunch. In the afternoon your manager walks you through the team, the project and your 30-day plan. Your manager raises your access requests on day 1 so that tools are ready by day 2.

## Your buddy
Every new joiner is paired with a buddy, an experienced colleague from the same team. Your buddy meets you for 30 minutes every day in the first week and then once a week for the first three months. Buddies answer day-to-day questions about tools, people and how things work, so ask them anything.

## Orientation sessions
There are three orientation sessions in week 1. Company and values is on Tuesday, security awareness is on Wednesday, and engineering practices is on Thursday. The security awareness session is mandatory, and you must complete the quiz within 5 days of joining.

## First 1:1
Your manager holds a 1:1 with you at the end of week 1 to review how the week went and agree your goals for the first month. After that, 1:1s happen every two weeks.

## Lunch and facilities
The cafeteria is on the 2nd floor and lunch is subsidised. Your team usually takes the first lunch with you on day 1.
