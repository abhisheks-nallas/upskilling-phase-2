# IT Setup and Equipment

## Laptop
You receive a company laptop on day 1 from the IT desk on the ground floor. Laptops are encrypted and managed by IT, so you cannot install software with admin rights. If you need a tool that is not in the self-service catalogue, raise a request with IT and expect an answer in 2 working days.

## Accounts and sign-in
Your company email and single sign-on account are created before you join. You must set up multi-factor authentication with the authenticator app on your first login, and SMS codes are not accepted. Use the company password manager for every work password. Passwords are never shared in chat or email.

## VPN
The VPN is required to reach the staging environment and internal wikis from outside the office. Install the VPN client from the self-service catalogue and sign in with your SSO account. If the VPN disconnects repeatedly, restart the client first, then contact the helpdesk.

## IT helpdesk
The helpdesk is open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 IST. Raise a ticket in the helpdesk portal or message the #it-help channel. Urgent issues such as a lost laptop must be reported by phone on the IT hotline immediately, and also to the security team.

## Returning equipment
When you leave, return your laptop and access cards to IT on your last working day. Equipment that is damaged through misuse may be charged back, and normal wear is not.
