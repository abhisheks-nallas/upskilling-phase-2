# Learning and Growth

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of INR 30,000 per year for courses, books and certifications. The budget is available from the day you join. Courses need manager approval before purchase, and certificates must be uploaded to the learning portal on completion.

## Protected learning time
Everyone gets 4 hours a week of protected learning time, which should be blocked in your calendar. Managers do not schedule meetings in that slot. Learning time is not carried over if unused.

## AI upskilling programme
The AI upskilling programme runs in phases of several weeks each. Participants follow a weekly course plan, build a small project along the way and present it at a milestone review. Developers build working code, and QA, BA and PM colleagues test and evaluate the same features.

## Certifications
The company pays the exam fee for one cloud or professional certification per year. If you leave within 12 months of a paid certification, the fee has to be repaid.

## Internal talks
Every second Friday at 16:00 there is a 45-minute internal tech talk. Any employee can volunteer to speak, and new joiners are encouraged to present their onboarding project after their first 90 days.
