# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every employee gets 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. New joiners get the leave pro-rated from their joining month, so someone joining in July gets 9 days for the first year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and anything above that lapses on 31 December.

## Sick leave
You get 10 days of paid sick leave a year. A medical certificate is needed only when you are off for more than 2 days in a row. Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence, by message or call.

## Casual leave
There are 6 days of casual leave a year for short personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave to take more than 10 days off in a row without manager approval.

## How to apply
Apply in the HR portal under "Leave". Annual leave needs at least 5 working days of notice, and your manager approves or rejects it within 2 working days. Leave of 3 days or longer during a project release week needs your manager and the delivery lead to agree.

## Public holidays and parental leave
The company follows a calendar of 12 public holidays that HR publishes every January. Maternity leave is 26 weeks and paternity leave is 10 working days, both fully paid.
