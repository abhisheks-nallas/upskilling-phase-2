# Office and Facilities

## Access cards and building hours
The office is open from 07:30 to 21:00 on working days. Your access card is issued on day 1 and works for the main entrance, your floor and the cafeteria. Weekend entry needs approval from your manager and a request to facilities at least one working day before. Never lend your card to anyone, and report a lost card to the front desk the same day so it can be blocked.

## Visitors
Visitors must be registered at the front desk by the employee hosting them, with the visitor's name and company, at least one hour before arrival. Visitors wear a badge, are escorted at all times and cannot enter the engineering floors without an employee. Client visitors are welcome in the meeting rooms on the first floor, and no customer systems are shown on screens that visitors can see.

## Meeting rooms
Book meeting rooms through the calendar. A booking is released automatically if nobody checks in within 10 minutes. The large rooms on the first floor are reserved on Tuesdays and Thursdays for the anchor-day planning sessions, so avoid booking them for other meetings on those days. Clean up the room and switch off the display when you leave.

## Cafeteria and refreshments
The cafeteria serves lunch from 12:30 to 14:30 and snacks at 16:30. Lunch is subsidised and the remaining cost is deducted from payroll at INR 60 per meal on days you check in with your card. Tea, coffee and water are free on every floor. Food is not allowed in the server room and the labs.

## Parking and transport
Two-wheeler parking is free and four-wheeler parking needs a permit, which you request from facilities after confirmation. A shuttle runs from the nearest metro station to the office every 20 minutes between 08:00 and 10:30 and again from 17:00 to 19:30.

## Health and safety
First aiders have a green badge. Fire drills are held every six months and everyone must take part. In an evacuation use the stairs, never the lifts, and go to the assembly point in the car park behind the building.
