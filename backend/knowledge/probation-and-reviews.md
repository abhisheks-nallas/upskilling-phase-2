# Probation and Performance Reviews

## Probation period
New employees have a probation period of 6 months. During probation the notice period is 15 days on either side, and after confirmation it becomes 60 days. Annual leave can be taken during probation, but not more than 5 days in a row.

## Check-ins
Your manager holds structured check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days. Each check-in covers what you delivered, what you learned and what support you need. The 90-day check-in is written down and shared with HR.

## Confirmation
HR confirms your employment in writing after the 6-month review if your manager's assessment is positive. Probation can be extended once, by up to 3 months, if there are open concerns, and you are told the reasons in writing.

## Annual review cycle
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and in October. The review has a self-assessment, a manager assessment and feedback from two peers you choose. Salary revisions and promotions are decided after the October cycle and take effect from 1 January.

## Feedback
Managers are expected to give feedback regularly, not only at review time. You can also ask for feedback from your buddy or any senior colleague at any point.
