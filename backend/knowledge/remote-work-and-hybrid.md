# Remote Work and Hybrid Policy

## Anchor days and office presence
Teams agree a weekly rhythm, but Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days when the whole team is expected in the office, because planning sessions, design reviews and most client workshops are held then. Outside the anchor days you can work from home if your manager agrees and your work does not need lab equipment or a shared meeting room. Managers may ask a team to come in on another day for a release, an audit or a client visit, and they should give at least two working days of notice for that.

## Work from another city
After you are confirmed, you can work from a different city in India for up to 20 days in a calendar year. Ask your manager at least 10 working days in advance and put the dates and the address in the HR portal. You need a stable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps and a quiet place for client calls. The days are counted per calendar year and do not roll over. If your manager approves but the project later needs you on site, the project need wins and the days are not used up.

## Work from abroad
Working from another country is not allowed by default, because it can create tax and data protection obligations for the company and for you. Exceptions need written approval from both HR and finance, and the request must be made at least 8 weeks before the trip. A short holiday abroad does not count as working there, but you must not log in to customer systems from a foreign country unless the approval covers it.

## Home office allowance
Once confirmed, you get a one-time allowance of INR 15,000 for a desk, chair or similar furniture. Claim it in the finance portal with the invoice within 60 days of purchase. Employees on the hybrid arrangement also get INR 1,000 a month towards their home internet bill, paid with the monthly payroll after you upload the bill. Both allowances stop on the last working day.

## Security when working remotely
Always use the company VPN when you are on a public or shared Wi-Fi network, and never work with customer data on a personal device. Lock your screen whenever you step away, even at home. Do not take client calls in a cafe or other public place, and use a headset so that others in the house cannot hear customer information. Report a lost phone or laptop at once through the IT hotline.

## Meetings and availability
Your calendar should show when you are in focus time, in a meeting or out of office. Keep your camera on for client calls and for the daily team stand-up, and use the status in the chat tool to show when you are away. Core hours still apply when you work remotely.
