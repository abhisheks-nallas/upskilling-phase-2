# Who to Contact

## By topic
Use the right channel to get a quick answer. For laptop, VPN or login problems, use the IT helpdesk and the #it-help channel. For leave, payroll and insurance, use the HR portal or the #ask-hr channel. For expense claims, use the finance portal. For security concerns, use #security-incident.

## Your manager and buddy
Your manager owns your goals, approvals and performance. Your buddy is the person for everyday questions such as which meeting to join or where to find a document. If you are unsure whom to ask, ask your buddy first.

## Escalation
If an issue is not solved within 2 working days, raise it with your manager. If the issue is about your manager or something you are uncomfortable raising with them, contact the HR business partner for your team directly.

## Workplace concerns
The company has a confidential ethics line, open 24 hours, for harassment, discrimination or fraud concerns. Reports can be made anonymously, and retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is not tolerated.

## Emergencies at the office
In a medical or fire emergency, call the front desk on the extension printed on your access card. First aiders are marked with a green badge on each floor, and the assembly point is the car park behind the building.
