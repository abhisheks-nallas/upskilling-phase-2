# Working Hours and Remote Work

## Core hours
Everyone is expected to be available between 11:00 and 16:00 IST on working days. Outside core hours you can start and finish when it suits you, as long as you complete 8 hours a day. Meetings with other teams are only scheduled inside core hours.

## Remote work
New joiners work from the office for their first two weeks so that onboarding sessions and buddy check-ins happen in person. After that you can work remotely up to 2 days a week. Your manager approves any change to this pattern, and fully remote roles are agreed in writing at the time of the offer.

## Timesheets
Log your hours in the timesheet tool every Friday before 18:00. Timesheets that are not submitted by Monday 12:00 are flagged to your manager. Billable and non-billable time use separate codes, and your manager will tell you which project code to use in your first week.

## Overtime and on-call
Overtime is not paid by default. If a delivery needs weekend work, your manager must approve it in advance and you receive compensatory leave of one day for every full day worked. On-call rotations only start after your first 90 days.
